# ConTech BOQ Estimator Skill

**Goal**: Transform raw CAD extractions and specification books into quantified, audit-ready Excel and Procore cost estimation spreadsheets.

## Workflow:
1. **Quantity Computation**: Calculate exact surface areas ($m^2$), volumes ($m^3$), rebar tonnages ($t$), and linear lengths ($m$).
2. **Deterministic Physics Validation**: Run Eurocode (EN 1992 / EN 1997) or USACE formulas to guarantee zero arithmetic errors.
3. **MasterFormat & UniFormat Mapping**: Map every line item to standard CSI 16/50 Division codes.
4. **Unit Rate & Variance Analysis**: Link items to historical unit rate databases and calculate bid variance ranges.
