# Five Sales Scripts

These are templates. Adapt only from observed customer facts.

## Script 1: First contact

"Hi [Name], this is [Rep] with [Business]. I am reaching out because [evidence-backed reason]. Is now a bad time for a 30-second question?"

## Script 2: Qualification

"Before I suggest anything, can I ask how you currently handle [specific lead/sales problem]?"

## Script 3: Bottleneck discovery

"Where do opportunities most often stall: getting the lead, reaching the lead, qualifying the lead, or following up?"

## Script 4: Offer transition

"Based on what you told me, the next step I would propose is [specific action]. It would [specific benefit that is supported by the audit], and we would keep [human approval boundary] in place."

## Script 5: Follow-up

"Hi [Name], following up on [specific observed issue]. I have the next step ready: [specific deliverable]. Reply [CTA] and I will send it over."

## Guardrails

Never claim a guaranteed revenue increase. Never imply that a call, message, or lead was verified unless there is evidence. Keep customer data out of public templates.
