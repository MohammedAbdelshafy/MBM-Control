# Five Sales Scripts

Adapt these templates only from observed customer facts.

## 1. First contact

"Hi [Name], this is [Rep] with [Business]. I am reaching out because [evidence-backed reason]. Is now a bad time for a 30-second question?"

## 2. Qualification

"Before I suggest anything, can I ask how you currently handle [specific lead or sales problem]?"

## 3. Bottleneck discovery

"Where do opportunities most often stall: getting the lead, reaching the lead, qualifying the lead, or following up?"

## 4. Offer transition

"Based on what you told me, the next step I would propose is [specific action]. It would [evidence-backed benefit], and we would keep [human approval boundary] in place."

## 5. Follow-up

"Hi [Name], following up on [specific observed issue]. I have the next step ready: [specific deliverable]. Reply [CTA] and I will send it over."

## Guardrails

Never promise a guaranteed revenue increase. Never imply a call, lead, or message was verified without evidence. Keep customer data out of public templates.
