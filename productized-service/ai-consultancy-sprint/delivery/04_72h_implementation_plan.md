# 72-Hour Implementation Plan

## Hour 0–2: intake

- Confirm business, market, services, current lead sources, and sales process.
- Capture evidence sources and identify unknowns.

## Hour 2–8: workflow design

- Select one evidence-backed workflow.
- Define trigger, inputs, outputs, human approval point, and failure state.
- Identify required credential classes without collecting secrets in this document.

## Hour 8–24: prototype

- Build the smallest testable version.
- Use sandbox or local mocks for external dependencies.
- Record observed outputs.

## Hour 24–48: QA

- Test expected and failure paths.
- Confirm no fabricated success state.
- Confirm external side effects remain blocked until explicit approval.

## Hour 48–72: handoff

- Deliver the audit, scripts, lead map, and implementation plan.
- Record what is READY, BLOCKED, or UNKNOWN.
- Define the next human-approved action.

## Acceptance

The sprint is complete only when the agreed artifacts are delivered and each material claim has evidence or is explicitly marked UNKNOWN.
