# AI Sprint Audit Kit

Customer delivery pack for the **AI Consultancy Sprint Audit**.

## What is included

1. AI Growth Audit
2. Five Sales Scripts
3. Lead Map Template
4. 72-Hour Implementation Plan

## Use

Complete the workbook with the customer's real business facts. Replace bracketed fields with observed evidence. Do not invent traffic, conversion, revenue, lead quality, or customer outcomes.

## Delivery boundary

This pack is a human-delivered artifact. It does not send email, SMS, calls, payments, or publishing actions.
