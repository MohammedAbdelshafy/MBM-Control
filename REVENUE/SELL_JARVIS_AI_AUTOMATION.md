# Jarvis AI Automation Sprint

## Commercial offer

A founder-led AI automation sprint for US, Canadian, Swiss, UK and European businesses.

### What we sell
- AI agents for repetitive operational workflows
- LLM integrations and internal copilots
- Lead/data intelligence and enrichment workflows
- Python/API automation
- Reporting and QA automation
- Customer-support and internal knowledge agents
- AI creative/content pipelines for ecommerce and marketing teams

### Delivery
1. Identify one revenue or operations bottleneck.
2. Map the existing workflow and systems.
3. Build one production workflow/agent.
4. Add logging, QA, fallback and handoff.
5. Deliver documentation and a short operating walkthrough.

### Pilot pricing
- Starter diagnostic: $250
- 7-14 day paid pilot: $1,500-$3,500
- Production implementation: $4,000-$12,000+
- Ongoing automation/AI operations: $1,000-$3,000+/month

Pricing is a commercial starting point and should be adapted to scope.

### Positioning
Do not sell "AI consulting" as an abstract service. Sell one measurable workflow improvement:
- reduce manual lead qualification
- automate reporting
- turn inbound questions into qualified opportunities
- automate document/data processing
- produce and version marketing creatives
- connect existing tools into one reliable AI workflow
