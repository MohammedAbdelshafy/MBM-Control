# OpsPilot AI

## 14-Day AI Workflow Transformation Sprint

**Positioning:** We do not sell vague AI consulting. We identify one costly, repetitive workflow and turn it into a reliable AI-powered operating system around the tools a client already uses.

### Best-fit buyers
US, Canada, UK, Switzerland, and European businesses with repetitive operations in sales, support, research, reporting, data processing, logistics, marketing, or internal knowledge.

### What the client gets
- Workflow discovery and bottleneck map
- One production AI agent or automation workflow
- API/webhook/database integrations
- LLM/tool orchestration
- Validation, logging, QA and failure handling
- Human approval gates where decisions require review
- Documentation and handoff
- Before/after operating metrics where the client can provide baseline data

### Typical use cases
- Lead qualification and data enrichment
- Research and reporting automation
- Internal knowledge / support agent
- Document intake and structured-data extraction
- CRM and operational-system synchronization
- AI-assisted customer or sales workflows
- Content/creative production pipelines
- Technical QA and evidence collection

### Delivery stack
Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, SQL, REST APIs, webhooks, GitHub, Supabase, n8n, Make/Zapier where appropriate, OpenAI/Claude and other LLM APIs, RAG/MCP patterns, React/full-stack components, logging and QA controls.

### Why this is different
1. Business problem first, model second.
2. Reuse the existing stack whenever practical.
3. Build measurable workflow improvements instead of demos.
4. Keep humans in control of commercially sensitive decisions.
5. Ship a maintainable system with documentation, tests and failure handling.
