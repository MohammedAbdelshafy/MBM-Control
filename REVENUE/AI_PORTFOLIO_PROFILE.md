## Engineering approach

I treat AI automation as a systems problem:

1. map the business workflow
2. identify the highest-value bottleneck
3. choose deterministic automation, AI, or a hybrid
4. integrate tools, APIs, and data
5. add validation, logging, retries, and human controls
6. measure the result
7. document the system for handoff and maintenance
