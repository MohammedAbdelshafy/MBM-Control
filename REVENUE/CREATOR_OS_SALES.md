# CreatorOS Sales Asset

## Offer
CreatorOS is an Instagram-first creator operating system for source videos, clip/edit/caption production, publishing workflow, analytics, and a future creator-owned community layer.

## Buyer
Independent creators, influencers, coaches, entertainers, educators, podcasters, and niche media personalities who publish frequently and want their audience to have a dedicated home.

## Demo
https://app.buildy.so/user-a15eae7d523949b1b29dca66/creatoros

## Product story
1. Creator adds a source video or content idea.
2. The workflow becomes a repeatable Clip -> Edit -> Caption -> Publish -> Learn pipeline.
3. Analytics closes the loop so the creator can learn what performs.
4. Community layer gives followers a creator-specific destination for exclusive drops, requests, Q&A, discussion, and future membership products.

## Technical evidence
- MBM-Social: multi-brand content operating system with clipping, captions, visual/hook/ranking/publishing workers, event bus, mission queue, scheduling, recovery, and multi-platform publishing.
- Jarvis MBM: orchestration and operational control for structured missions and sales workflows.
- Buildy CreatorOS: live customer-facing demo shell for the creator product concept.

## Sales positioning
Lead with a personalized creator workflow, not generic AI.
Do not claim Instagram API automation or full production video generation inside Buildy unless those integrations are actually connected.
Use the demo to start a discovery conversation around the creator's current workflow and their audience/community needs.

## Outreach rule
Human approval remains authoritative for outbound campaigns and commercial commitments. Do not use Contec company email.
