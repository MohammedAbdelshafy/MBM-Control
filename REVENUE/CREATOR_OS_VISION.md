# CreatorOS Product Vision

CreatorOS is a creator-owned AI operating system and future community hub.

## What it helps with
- Source-video intake and content planning
- Clip selection and editing workflow
- Captions, hooks, titles, descriptions, thumbnails and repurposing
- Publishing and cross-platform scheduling
- Content calendar and campaign planning
- Performance analytics and recommendations
- Audience/content insights and experiment tracking
- Sponsor/brand-deal pipeline and meeting preparation
- Meeting scheduling, notes, follow-ups and action tracking
- Inbox/DM triage concepts
- Asset and brand-kit organization
- Collaboration with editors, social managers and assistants
- Product, membership, course, merch and community launch workflows
- Follower requests, Q&A, exclusive drops and community spaces

## Visual direction
The product should feel alive: a dark-to-bright editorial canvas, colorful creator cards, animated ambient AI motion in the background, floating content nodes, subtle waveform/video motifs, and real creator imagery. Motion should be calm and premium, not distracting.

## Architecture evidence
MBM-Social provides the production-system evidence for clipping, captioning, visual/hook/ranking workers, publishing, analytics/recovery and multi-platform orchestration.
Jarvis MBM provides orchestration and operational-control evidence.

## Important truth boundary
The current Buildy demo is a customer-facing concept shell. It does not by itself perform native AI video generation, Instagram API publishing, or autonomous meeting operations unless those integrations are actually connected.
