# MBM-Control Commercialization Surface

MBM-Control is the operator-facing execution surface for the MBM sales/dialer workflow.

## Productized offer
Dialer Enablement / Cold Calling Assistant pilot.

## Deliverable
- eligibility and suppression checks
- prioritized calling queue
- call workspace
- disposition capture
- follow-up creation
- audit trail
- manager exception view

## Commercial boundary
Calling remains human-approved. The UI must never imply that a configured provider, number, credential, or auto-dial capability exists unless it is verified in the runtime.

## Expansion
- additional seats
- workflow configuration
- reporting/delivery digest
- revenue recovery
- AI call intelligence for authorized transcripts/recordings

## Pricing
Existing setup/subscription amounts are planning hypotheses only and must be validated before publication.
