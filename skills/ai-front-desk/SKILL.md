# AI Front Desk
1. Define channels, business hours, approved knowledge, calendar rules, and escalation paths.
2. Capture inbound intent and customer-provided details.
3. Answer only from approved business knowledge.
4. Qualify against explicit rules and offer booking or human handoff.
5. Record interaction and next action in approved CRM.
6. Escalate ambiguous, sensitive, complaint, refund, emergency, or regulated questions.
7. Measure response time, booking rate, handoff rate, and failures.
Guardrails: do not invent availability, pricing, reviews, credentials, or outcomes. Do not message or change calendars without required permission.
