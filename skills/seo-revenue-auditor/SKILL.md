# SEO Revenue Auditor
1. Confirm property and analysis period.
2. Retrieve current performance data from the connected analytics source.
3. Segment by page, query, device, country, and time where available.
4. Identify CTR opportunities, ranking clusters, content decay, cannibalization, and indexing issues.
5. Tie findings to observed metrics and label assumptions.
6. Prioritize by impact hypothesis, effort, and evidence strength.
7. Define baseline and monitoring plan.
Guardrails: do not invent traffic/rankings or equate impressions with revenue.
