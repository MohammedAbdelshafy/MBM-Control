# Real Estate Deal Packet
1. Define geography, filters, and buyer criteria.
2. Acquire only authorized public or licensed data.
3. Normalize property identity and deduplicate.
4. Attach source citations and retrieval dates.
5. Calculate transparent rule-based grades for stated criteria.
6. Keep each criterion visible for audit.
7. Separate property signals from seller intent; verify intent independently.
8. Return buyer-ready packet and prioritized review queue.
Guardrails: do not fabricate phone numbers, emails, motivation, or legal contactability. Do not initiate outreach automatically.
