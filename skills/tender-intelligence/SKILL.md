# Tender Intelligence
1. Confirm tender files and project scope.
2. Extract project metadata, BOQ items, trades, quantities, specifications, and drawing references.
3. Normalize units and classify trade packages.
4. Flag duplicates, discrepancies, scope gaps, and ambiguities.
5. Estimate costs only from supplied price sources or clearly labeled assumptions.
6. Produce a risk register, missing-data list, and bid report.
Guardrails: do not fabricate prices, quantities, approvals, or market data. Human review is required before bid submission.
