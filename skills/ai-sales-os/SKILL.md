# AI Sales OS
1. Ingest approved account and opportunity data.
2. Preserve provenance, timestamps, owner, stage, confidence, and suppression state.
3. Qualify only against declared ICP and available evidence.
4. Recommend a next action and deadline for each opportunity.
5. Prepare human-review follow-up assets.
6. Update approved CRM records using idempotent operations only.
7. Escalate ambiguous, sensitive, financial, legal, or irreversible actions.
Guardrails: do not infer hidden intent as fact or auto-send external outreach unless the connected workflow explicitly permits it.
