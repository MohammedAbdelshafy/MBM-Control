# MBM Revenue Execution Schedule

Canonical source: GitHub repository `MohammedAbdelshafy/MBM-Control`.

## Daily loop
1. Discover fresh buyer pain and demand signals.
2. Package one existing MBM asset into a concrete offer.
3. Verify the company and intended buyer before outreach.
4. Produce one buyer-facing proof/demo/content asset.
5. Execute tailored outreach using approved connected channels.
6. Record replies, meetings, objections, payment status, and delivery status.
7. Convert successful pilots into recurring offers.
8. Update this repository with evidence and blockers.

## Weekly loop
- Review every revenue-capable repository asset.
- Kill or pause offers with no meaningful buyer signal.
- Refresh ICP, proof, pricing, and outreach language from actual responses.
- Publish a case study or product proof from completed work.
- Preserve provenance and never claim a sale, qualified buyer, publication, or deployment without evidence.

## Guardrails
- Revenue validation before non-blocking engineering.
- Verify contacts before personal-contact outreach.
- No fabricated customer evidence.
- High-risk domains require appropriate human review and safety escalation.
