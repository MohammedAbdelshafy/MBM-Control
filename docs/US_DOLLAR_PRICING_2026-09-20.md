## Why these prices
- Current Upwork guidance puts AI-assisted short-form editing around $100-$600/project for 3-10 clips, and broader branded social packages around $500-$2,000/project. The $399 pilot sits inside that existing buying range while bundling workflow handling rather than selling raw editing hours.
- Current AI integration work on Upwork is commonly priced around $1,000-$4,000 for a single integration and $2,500-$9,000 for chatbot/copilot integration. A $750 pilot is intentionally a smaller validation engagement, not a full production integration.
- Commodity email verification is much cheaper than a DFY data-operations outcome. Current public tools advertise roughly $40-$65 for 10,000 records, while some providers are substantially higher. MBM should sell the classified, normalized, suppression-ready business output and handoff, not compete on verification credits.
- $497 is positioned as a low-friction business purchase while preserving room for delivery effort and upsell.

## Sales rule
Lead with one sentence:
"Send us the messy workflow or file, and we'll run a fixed-scope AI-assisted pilot with human QA and a measurable before/after."

Do not sell the underlying models, credits, prompts, or hours.
Sell the finished business outcome.

## Payment rule
All US-facing proposals, quotes, invoices, and direct checkout pages use USD.
Do not describe an EGP-priced Shopify draft as a USD checkout.

## Shopify state
The connected Shopify store is currently on a trial plan and its store currency is EGP. Shopify's official documentation says multi-currency checkout requires Shopify Payments or Adyen, and market currency can be configured for a US market. Because the current connected store does not expose a currency/market mutation through the available connector, the three Shopify products remain staging drafts rather than being represented as live USD checkout products.
