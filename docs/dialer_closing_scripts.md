# MBM Dialer Closing Scripts

## Core close
"Based on what you told me, I think this could make sense for both of us. If I can get you a fair number and make the process easy, are you open to moving forward?"

## Price close
"What number would make you comfortable selling it?"

If they give a number:
"If I can get close to that and make the process easy, are you ready to move forward?"

## Thinking-it-over close
"Totally fair. What's the main thing you'd need to know before you'd feel comfortable saying yes?"

## Appointment close
"Let's make this easy. How about we spend 10 minutes going over the property and numbers, and then you can decide?"

## Motivated-seller close
"It sounds like getting this property off your hands is important. If I can solve that without making this complicated, would you be comfortable getting started?"

## No-pressure close
"There's no pressure either way. I just want to see whether the numbers make sense for you. If they do, great. If they don't, no problem. Want to see what I can offer?"

## Wholesaler / investor close
"What kind of deal are you looking to move right now?"

Then:
"If the numbers work, can we lock down the next step today?"

## Best three-line close
1. "Sounds like there's a real opportunity here."
2. "If the numbers make sense, are you ready to move forward?"
3. "What would stop you from doing it today?"

## Offer request
"Absolutely. To make sure I don't waste your time, what price were you hoping to get?"

Then:
"If the offer lands where you need it, are you comfortable moving forward?"

## Spouse / partner objection
"Of course. What do you think they'll want to know?"

Then:
"Great. Let's get that answered now so you can have a real conversation with them."

## Operator guidance
- Ask one question at a time.
- Listen before responding.
- Move toward one concrete next step: offer, appointment, property details, or agreed follow-up time.
- Do not pressure or misrepresent the offer, financing, timeline, or intent.
- Respect a clear no and any request not to be contacted again.
