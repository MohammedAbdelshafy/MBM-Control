# MBM Revenue Factory: Sell the Workflow, Not the AI

## Buyer-facing one-liner
Turn one recurring business workflow into a measurable automation pilot without forcing a platform replacement.

## Current offers

### 1. Short-form Content Distribution Pilot
For podcasters, media companies, creator teams, agencies, publishers, and expert-led brands.

**Workflow:** long-form source → clip candidates → captions → QA → content calendar → scheduled distribution → performance feedback.

**Pilot:** one real workflow, fixed scope, before/after measurement.

**Measure:** turnaround time, usable outputs, scheduled posts, manual handoffs removed.

### 2. Visual AI Evidence Pilot
For warranty/service, returns, field inspection, construction QA, and support teams.

**Workflow:** photo/screenshot/video → structured evidence → issue candidates → missing-context questions → safe next action → human escalation.

**Pilot:** one narrow intake/review workflow with explicit uncertainty and audit trail.

### 3. P4 Lead Cleaner DFY
For teams carrying stale, duplicate, incomplete, or operationally unreliable B2B data.

**Workflow:** ingest → normalize → dedupe → validate → classify → export/route.

**Pilot:** one representative dataset with deterministic before/after quality checks.

### 4. AI Business Systems Sprint
For SMB operators with a repetitive finance, sales, operations, reporting, support, or admin workflow.

**Workflow:** map one bottleneck → automate the smallest useful slice → handoff/document → measure saved effort.

### 5. Construction / BOQ / Field-QA Pilot
For contractors, developers, estimators, and project-control teams.

**Workflow:** drawings/documents/photos/notes → structured takeoff or QA evidence → exceptions → review queue.

### 6. Developer / Agent / MCP Implementation
For teams that need an AI workflow connected to their existing tools without committing to a large transformation.

**Workflow:** discovery → architecture → smallest working integration → validation → handoff.

### 7. Productized Creator / Business OS Packages
Existing templates, dashboards, calculators, operating systems, and workflow products can be sold as one-time digital products or implementation packages where the delivery path is verified.

## Current demand signals

Fresh September 2026 research indicates continued demand for practical AI implementation and workflow automation. The U.S. Chamber highlights AI/technology-oriented business opportunities in 2026, while Shopify's August 28, 2026 AI-business guide emphasizes service-based AI implementation, automation consulting, and AI content businesses as accessible models. Current small-business social guidance also frames social as a connected growth engine and notes that managing multiple networks natively can become an operational burden.

These sources are market context, not proof that any specific prospect will buy.

## Highest-priority niches

1. Media/podcast/editorial teams with recurring video output.
2. Creator agencies and social-content production teams.
3. Warranty/returns/service operations with photo intake.
4. Construction inspection and documentation workflows.
5. SMB operations teams with manual reporting or repetitive handoffs.
6. B2B teams with stale CRM/list data.

## Commercial rule

Sell a fixed-scope pilot first. Do not sell speculative software capability. Do not claim a buyer is qualified merely because a database returns an intent signal.

## Revenue gate

DEMAND SIGNAL → BUSINESS HYPOTHESIS → DISCOVERY → PAID PILOT → PROOF → RECURRING OFFER

No sale, payment, publication, or capability is marked complete without evidence.
