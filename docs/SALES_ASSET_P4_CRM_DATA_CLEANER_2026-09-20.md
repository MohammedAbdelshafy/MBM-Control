# P4 Lead Cleaner DFY — CRM Data Cleanup Offer

## The offer

**Clean one messy sales dataset for a fixed fee: $497 / $997 / $1,497 depending on scope.**

## Best-fit workflow

Teams with recurring CRM or lead-data problems such as:
- duplicate records
- inconsistent fields
- stale or unusable rows
- formatting and normalization work
- manual reconciliation between lists and CRM exports
- recurring reporting preparation

## Delivery

1. Inspect a sample and agree on the cleaning rules.
2. Normalize the agreed fields.
3. Deduplicate using documented rules.
4. Flag records that require human review.
5. Return the cleaned dataset plus a short QA summary.

## Buyer CTA

Send a representative sample and describe the fields that cause the most manual work.

## Integrity note

The offer is documented as a sellable service. No customer outcome, accuracy percentage, or completed delivery is claimed here.
