# MBM GTM Outreach Playbook — US B2B — 2026-09-20

## Primary motion

Target senior operators with an explicit automation/workflow signal and a verifiable professional contact.

### Message architecture

1. Observed workflow signal
2. Specific repetitive workflow hypothesis
3. Fixed $750 pilot
4. Low-friction CTA

### Sales email template

Hi {{first_name}},

I’m reaching out because {{company}} appears to be investing in {{workflow_signal}}.

We run a fixed-scope AI Workflow Pilot: one recurring workflow, AI-assisted execution, human QA, and a before/after measurement.

The pilot is $750 and the goal is simple: prove whether one workflow is worth automating before anyone commits to a larger system.

Would a 20-minute workflow review be useful?

Best,
MBM

## Vertical angles

### Sales enablement
Focus on intake, qualification, CRM updates, handoffs and reporting.

### Engineering / QA
Focus on repetitive ticket, documentation, triage, release or QA handoffs.

### Reporting / operations
Focus on recurring manual reporting, data preparation and approval workflows.

### Service operations
Focus on intake, triage, evidence collection and routing.

## Follow-up sequence

Follow-up 1:
- reference the workflow only
- ask for the smallest useful starting point

Follow-up 2:
- provide a one-sentence pilot scope
- offer a direct scheduling CTA

Stop when:
- recipient opts out
- contact is invalid
- relevance is no longer defensible

## Qualification state

Do not mark a prospect as "qualified buyer" merely because:
- they match an ICP
- an intent score is high
- their email is valid
- an email was sent

Buyer qualification requires an attributable interaction showing relevant problem context.

## Reply routing

Positive reply -> conversation
Needs details -> send one-page pilot scope
Price objection -> diagnose scope/value before changing price
No reply -> limited follow-up, then move to nurture
Opt-out -> suppress
Invalid/catch-all -> do not direct-send
