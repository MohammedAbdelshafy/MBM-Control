### The five-step pilot
1. **Baseline** — map the current workflow and bottleneck.
2. **Pilot** — introduce AI-assisted steps around the existing process.
3. **Human QA** — keep a review gate where judgment matters.
4. **Measure** — compare the agreed baseline with the pilot.
5. **Decide** — keep, expand, change or stop based on evidence.

### Deliverable
