## Buyer message

Do not sell “AI.” Sell the removed handoff, faster turnaround, lower manual effort, or clearer operational decision.

Example:

> Give us one real workflow. We run a fixed-scope pilot and measure the before/after. If it does not remove enough manual work to justify expansion, we stop there.
