## Important qualification rule
A company that manufactures a related product is a potential downstream user, not automatically a scrap buyer. Before quoting a buyer as qualified, confirm:
1. exact material and polymer type
2. form (offcuts, lumps, bale, granulated, etc.)
3. contamination level
4. monthly quantity available
5. collection/delivery radius
6. sampling/testing requirements
7. price basis and payment terms
8. recurring monthly quota

## Compliance
Keep finished branded/counterfeit footwear separate from ordinary material scrap. Prioritize unbranded/OEM material waste unless lawful disposition is confirmed.
