Find the weak spot in your lead and follow-up process.

Slow responses, incomplete records and inconsistent follow-up can cost a business opportunities. The size of the problem varies by team and industry, so we start by looking at your process and evidence rather than assuming a fixed loss.

Start with your current workflow. We’ll review the tools and lead sources you already use, identify useful improvements, and explain what can be delivered with the access and data available.

What the review covers

We check the actual inputs. We review the lead sources, handoffs and follow-up steps available for your business. Any verification claim is limited to the fields and checks actually run, and customer results are only reported after they are measured.
  • Source notes. We document where supplied records came from and which checks were actually run. We do not describe a list as phone-verified unless that check is supported by evidence.
  • Practical options. Recommendations are based on your current tools and permissions. A proposed integration is not treated as live until it is configured and tested.
  • Clear deliverables. We agree in writing on the output, timing, price and any refund terms before the work starts.

How the work proceeds

  1. Review — we discuss your process, target customers and current lead sources, then agree which details can be checked.
  2. Plan — we provide a written set of recommendations and an implementation outline for the agreed scope.
  3. Optional setup — any call, messaging, data or CRM integration is scoped, configured and tested separately before it is described as live.
  4. Ongoing support — available only if included in the agreed plan, with a clear list of what is monitored and maintained.
