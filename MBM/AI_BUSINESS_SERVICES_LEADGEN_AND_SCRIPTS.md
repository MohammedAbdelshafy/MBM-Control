# MBM AI Business Services LeadGen + Scripts Playbook

## Mission
Turn MBM's AI capability into owner-focused offers across accounting, finance, management, operations, sales, customer service, HR, procurement, inventory, reporting, and business intelligence.

This document is a planning/positioning layer. It does not contain scraped private contact lists or unverifiable claims.

## Core offer ladder

### 1. AI Finance & Accounting Control Layer
Ideal buyer: business owner / CFO / finance manager at an SMB or mid-market company.
Problems: bookkeeping workload, reconciliation delays, invoice processing, AP/AR visibility, cash-flow uncertainty, month-end reporting.
Offer: connect accounting/banking/invoice data, automate categorization and reconciliation workflows, produce management-ready reporting, surface exceptions for human review.
Outcome language: faster close, fewer manual touches, better cash visibility, cleaner management reporting.

### 2. AI CFO / Financial Intelligence
Ideal buyer: owner without a full finance leadership layer, or finance team that needs decision support.
Problems: cash planning, margin drift, customer profitability, scenario planning, budget variance.
Offer: weekly management brief, cash-flow forecast, margin/variance analysis, scenario model, exception alerts.

### 3. AI AP / AR & Collections
Ideal buyer: owner, controller, finance manager.
Problems: late invoices, forgotten follow-ups, vendor invoice processing, payment-status ambiguity.
Offer: invoice intake, approval routing, aging dashboard, collection-priority queue, reminder workflows, exception escalation.

### 4. AI Operations Command Center
Ideal buyer: COO, operations director, business owner.
Problems: repetitive admin, workflow bottlenecks, handoff failures, SOP drift, poor visibility.
Offer: map workflows, automate routine tasks, assign owners, surface bottlenecks, generate daily operating brief.

### 5. AI Management / KPI Cockpit
Ideal buyer: owner, CEO, general manager.
Problems: too many spreadsheets, delayed reporting, unclear priorities.
Offer: unified KPI dashboard, weekly management briefing, anomaly detection, action queue.

### 6. AI Sales & Customer Service Layer
Ideal buyer: owner, sales director, customer-service leader.
Problems: missed leads, slow response, inconsistent follow-up, repetitive support work.
Offer: lead intake, qualification, follow-up automation, call/email summaries, support triage, knowledge assistant.

### 7. AI Procurement & Inventory
Ideal buyer: owner, procurement manager, operations manager.
Problems: stockouts, excess inventory, manual purchasing, weak supplier visibility.
Offer: reorder alerts, supplier comparison workflow, purchase-request automation, inventory exceptions.

### 8. AI HR / People Operations
Ideal buyer: owner, HR manager, operations manager.
Problems: recruiting admin, onboarding, employee-document workflows, repetitive HR questions.
Offer: candidate intake, interview summaries, onboarding checklist automation, internal HR knowledge assistant.

### 9. AI Construction / Project Controls
Ideal buyer: construction-company owner, PM, QS/estimator.
Problems: BOQ processing, estimating, procurement, daily reporting, document search, change tracking.
Offer: BOQ extraction, pricing workflow, supplier database, estimate review, project-control dashboards, document assistant.

### 10. AI Business Operating System
Ideal buyer: owner running multiple departments.
Problems: fragmented systems and no single operating picture.
Offer: connect finance + sales + operations + management data into one decision layer with approvals, dashboards, alerts and AI assistants.

## Lead archetypes
Prioritize businesses with clear operational complexity and an owner who can approve a workflow improvement quickly.

Tier A:
- construction and contracting companies
- distributors and wholesalers
- multi-location service businesses
- professional-services firms
- clinics and healthcare-service businesses
- agencies with substantial recurring admin
- manufacturers with procurement/inventory complexity
- property-management and real-estate operating companies

Tier B:
- logistics and transport companies
- restaurants / hospitality groups
- ecommerce operators
- education/training businesses
- maintenance/service contractors
- specialty retailers

## Owner-first targeting
Use business/professional context rather than sensitive personal attributes.
Preferred roles:
- Owner
- Founder
- CEO
- Managing Director
- General Manager
- CFO / Finance Director
- COO / Operations Director
- Controller

Useful business signals:
- growing headcount
- multiple locations
- high transaction volume
- visible finance/operations hiring
- recent software migration
- expanding service area
- large inventory catalog
- procurement complexity
- recurring customer base
- active digital transformation initiatives

## Qualification model
Score 0-100:
- 25 pain intensity
- 20 workflow complexity
- 15 likely ROI
- 15 buying authority
- 10 timing signal
- 10 data/system readiness
- 5 strategic fit

High-priority lead threshold: 75+.

## Short-form content script template

HOOK:
"Your company may already have the data it needs. The problem is that nobody is turning it into decisions fast enough."

PAIN:
"Accounting has the numbers. Operations has the workflow. Sales has the customers. Management has five different spreadsheets."

SOLUTION:
"An AI business layer connects those systems, automates the repetitive work, and turns exceptions into an action queue."

OWNER VALUE:
"That means less manual admin, faster reporting, better visibility, and more time for decisions that actually move the business."

CTA:
"We can map one workflow in your business and show where AI can realistically save time or improve control."

## Vertical script examples

### Construction owner
HOOK: "If your estimator spends hours reading the same BOQ format, you have an AI opportunity sitting in front of you."
BODY: "An AI estimator can extract items, normalize quantities, compare supplier prices, flag missing information and prepare a management-ready estimate. Humans still approve the numbers. The AI removes the repetitive work."
CTA: "Show us one BOQ and we can map the workflow."

### Distributor owner
HOOK: "Your inventory problem may actually be a visibility problem."
BODY: "An AI operations layer can combine sales velocity, stock levels and purchasing data, then surface reorder risks and slow-moving inventory before they become expensive surprises."
CTA: "We can map your purchasing workflow and identify the highest-value automation first."

### Professional-services owner
HOOK: "Your team should not spend Monday morning building the report that tells you what happened last week."
BODY: "Connect the source systems, automate the reporting layer, and let AI summarize exceptions, pipeline movement, staffing pressure and cash signals into one management brief."
CTA: "We can design the first management cockpit around the systems you already use."

### Finance/accounting owner
HOOK: "Accounting software records transactions. It does not automatically run the business."
BODY: "AI can sit above the accounting system to reconcile exceptions, explain variances, monitor receivables, forecast cash and prepare decision-ready summaries."
CTA: "We can map the finance workflow and identify the first automation worth implementing."
