## 1. AI Receptionist
- **Problem:** Calls go unanswered during peak hours; after-hours callers hit voicemail.
- **Buyer:** Local service businesses, clinics, dental/medspa, home services.
- **Solution:** Voice agent answers, qualifies, books, routes, captures leads 24/7.
- **Deliverables:** Number/forwarding setup, qualification script, calendar booking, transcript log, weekly summary email.
- **Implementation time:** 5–10 business days.
- **Pricing model:** Setup + monthly retainer (per-location).
- **MRR option:** Yes (hosting, monitoring, script tuning).
- **Setup option:** One-time build fee.
- **Proof/demo:** Live demo line the buyer can call; call recordings from their own test calls.
- **Upsell path:** Missed-call recovery → lead qualification → CRM automation.

## 2. AI Missed-Call Recovery
- **Problem:** Missed inbound calls convert at near-zero via voicemail.
- **Buyer:** Any phone-first business (dental, contractors, salons, medspa).
- **Solution:** Missed-call detection → instant SMS + automated callback → booking link or warm transfer.
- **Deliverables:** Call-tracking hook, SMS templates (TCPA-aware consent flow), recovery dashboard, weekly recovered-call report.
- **Implementation time:** 3–7 business days.
- **Pricing model:** Setup + monthly; performance tier negotiable on measured recoveries only.
- **MRR option:** Yes.
- **Setup option:** Yes.
- **Proof/demo:** 48-hour pilot on their own missed-call log (with their baseline measured first).
- **Upsell path:** AI receptionist (primary-line answering), appointment follow-up.
