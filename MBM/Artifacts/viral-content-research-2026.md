## 1. Don't Watch This (@DONTWATCHTHIS1)
**Theme:** Dark stories, mystery, suspense, true crime lite
**KPIs:** 500K views/30d, 6% CTR, 45% avg view pct, 300 subs/post

### What's Viral Right Now
- **Unsolved mystery shorts** (60s case summaries) — highest RPM niche ($4-10 RPM)
- **"This case was never solved" open-ended format** → drives massive comment engagement
- **Cold case updates** (DNA breakthroughs, new evidence) — evergreen, serializable
- **Multi-part case deep dives** (Part 1/2/3) → subscriber growth cheat code
- **Criminal psychology** (profiling, forensic science) — educational angle, growing fast

### Top Viral Hooks
- "In 1996, a woman vanished from her locked apartment. The door was bolted from the inside."
- "This man robbed 26 banks and the FBI couldn't catch him for 12 years."
- "Three hikers went into these woods. Only one came back."

### Proven Formats
| Format | Description | Why It Works |
|---|---|---|
| 60s case summary | One case, start to finish | Standalone views, shareable |
| Open-ended mystery | No reveal, ask "what do you think?" | Comment engagement (algorithm signal) |
| Evidence breakdown | Single piece of forensic evidence | Educational, save-worthy |
| "Red flags everyone missed" | Hindsight warning signs | Shares & saves |

### Optimization
- **Script structure:** Hook (0-3s shocking detail) → Tension (3-40s breadcrumbs) → Cliffhanger/reveal (40-60s)
- **Post time:** Evenings (20:00-22:00 EST) — aligns with current schedule
- **Audio:** Dark ambient, suspenseful piano. Auto-captions mandatory (85% watch without sound)
- **Thumbnails:** Dark, desaturated. One shocked face or silhouette. Max 3 words, red accent.

- **Kid & pet duets** — family-safe, cross-generational appeal

## 5. Twists Revealed (@TwistsRevealed)
**Theme:** Plot twists, reveals, shocking stories, suspense
**KPIs:** 550K views/30d, 6.5% CTR, 48% avg view pct, 350 subs/post

### What's Viral Right Now
- **Micro-dramas** — complete narratives in under 60s (Disney+ is launching vertical feeds)
- **"It looked normal... until"** — setup/payoff framing dominates
- **AI-narrated story shorts** — sub-$3 production cost, high RPM
- **Movie/TV plot twist explainers** — trending titles drive search
- **Real-life shocking reveals** ("The neighbor was the killer the entire time")
- **Horror/sci-fi fiction shorts** — strong recurring audience

### Proven Formats
| Format | Description | Why It Works |
|---|---|---|
| Setup + twist reveal | Normal → shocking reveal | 100% retention if done right |
| "What happened to..." series | Multi-part deep dive | Subscriber growth |
| Movie twist explained | Breakdown of famous twists | Search traffic |
| Real-life shocking story | True twist from history | Viral shareability |
| "Red flags they ignored" | Hindsight warning signs | Comments + saves |

### Script Structure
1. **Hook (0-3s):** Establish normalcy or the setup
2. **Breadcrumbs (3-40s):** Drop details that raise questions
3. **The Twist (40-55s):** The reveal
4. **Cliffhanger/Question (55-60s):** "What would you have done?"

### Optimization
- **Title:** Setup + payoff framing. "It looked normal... until". 4-7 words. No spoilers.
- **Thumbnails:** Split — setup on left, "?" or shock on right. Tension colors. Never spoil.
- **Audio:** Suspenseful/soundtrack build-up. Auto-captions critical.
- **Post time:** Evenings (20:00-22:00)
- **Series:** Part 1/2/3 on complex stories drives subs
