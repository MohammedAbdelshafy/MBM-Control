# AI Commerce Platform Candidate

**Status:** DISCOVERED / EVIDENCE-GATED
**Source:** Ty video transcription supplied in conversation on 2026-09-13
**Factory routing:** Choice Factory → Product Factory → validation gate → build only after evidence

## Hypothesis
An AI-native commerce platform may compress storefront creation, content generation, experimentation, and commerce-backend operations into a natural-language workflow.

## Claims captured from source
- AI can generate many storefront variants.
- User can target a conversion-rate improvement.
- Product imagery can be used as a starting input for store creation.
- Natural-language requests can generate store content such as reviews/copy.
- Backend operations may include orders, products, and integrations.
- The platform may reduce reliance on traditional Shopify setup work.

## Evidence requirements
Do **not** treat marketing claims as validated facts. Before promotion to a build candidate, verify:
1. Exact platform/product identity and official URL.
2. Pricing and transaction/platform fees.
3. Store generation capability from product image/input.
4. Real variant-generation and experimentation capability, including traffic allocation and measurement.
5. Backend capabilities for catalog, orders, fulfillment, payments, and integrations.
6. Exportability/portability of storefront, data, domain, and customer records.
7. API/webhook support and authentication model.
8. Revenue evidence: distinguish gross revenue, profit, ad spend, refunds, and attributable platform impact.

## Factory role
This is an **option/capability**, not a product commitment. The Choice Factory may rank it against existing opportunities. The Product Factory may compile it only when the opportunity reaches the existing evidence gate.

## Revenue-first experiment
Preferred first test: manually validate one narrow commerce use case with a single product/store and measure time-to-live, conversion, operating cost, and first-dollar potential. Avoid building a generalized commerce platform before this evidence exists.

## Decision rule
- VERIFIED + economically attractive → advance to Product Factory.
- PLAUSIBLE but unverified → keep in discovery backlog.
- Marketing-only / weak economics → reject or archive.
