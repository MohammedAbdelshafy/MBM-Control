# Creator Partnership Loop

Creators are distribution partners and demand-validation partners, not interchangeable ad inventory.

## Lifecycle

```text
DISCOVER CREATOR
  ↓
QUALIFY AUDIENCE + TRUST + COMMERCIAL FIT
  ↓
INVITE WITH CLEAR VALUE + COMPENSATION
  ↓
SEND SAMPLE / DEMO ACCESS
  ↓
CREATOR REVIEWS PRODUCT
  ↓
COLLECT OBJECTIONS + FEEDBACK
  ↓
GENERATE CREATOR-NATIVE ASSETS
  ↓
PUBLISH WITH DISCLOSURE + TRACKING
  ↓
MEASURE CLICKS → CHECKOUTS → PURCHASES
  ↓
FEED LEARNINGS BACK INTO PRODUCT + OFFER
  ↓
RETAIN HIGH-FIT PARTNERS
```

## Creator pack requirements

Each qualified partner receives a structured pack containing:

- the audience problem being addressed
- the demonstrated outcome
- evidence/proof assets
- creator-native creative assets
- the tracking key
- the compensation model and commission rate
- a clear disclosure requirement
- feedback questions that can change the product or offer

Do not manufacture creator testimonials or imply endorsement before a creator has actually reviewed or used the product.

## Commercial loop

Creator economics should be measurable at the transaction level. Attribution should retain the opportunity, product, creator, channel, campaign, checkout and transaction identifiers when available.

A creator is retained when observed conversion, audience fit, trust, and feedback quality justify continuing the partnership. Reach alone is not a qualification criterion.

## Content system

The factory should produce creator-native variants rather than asking every creator to publish identical ads. The underlying claims stay consistent while the hook, demonstration, story structure, examples, and format adapt to the creator's audience.
